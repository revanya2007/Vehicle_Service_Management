# 🚗 Vehicle Service Management Application

## 📌 Project Overview

**Vehicle Service Management** is a Pega-based application developed for **Urban Fleet Operations** to manage the complete vehicle servicing process.

The application allows customers to raise vehicle service requests and enables service personnel to inspect vehicles, prepare service estimates, obtain customer approval, assign service work, track service progress, and complete the service request.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Vehicle service requests handled through phone calls and emails can cause:

- ⏳ Delays in service processing
- 📋 Poor tracking of service requests
- 🔍 Lack of service visibility
- 📚 Difficulty maintaining service history

This application provides a structured digital workflow to manage vehicle servicing from **request creation to completion**.

---

## 🚀 Objectives

- 📝 Allow customers to raise vehicle service requests
- 👤 Record customer information
- 🚘 Record vehicle information
- 🔍 Perform vehicle inspection
- 💰 Generate service estimates
- ✅ Capture customer approval
- 👨‍🔧 Assign service work to technicians
- 📊 Track service status
- 📧 Notify customers after service completion
- 📚 Maintain vehicle service history
